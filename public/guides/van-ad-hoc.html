What changed for requesters? Earlier → now
  • Two broad reasons → six specific choices: select company, vendor or ODCD absence/breakdown, or High Volume. Relevant options follow the station’s deployed fleet.
  • Company replacement: choose an owned van already marked Breakdown or Under service. If none qualify, contact Fleet to update the record; do not select another reason to bypass this.
  • High Volume: check the compact inbound summary, delivery history and approximate small/van split before requesting extra capacity.
  • Estimated CPS: the entered estimate and shipment count show the expected cost per shipment.
  • Tracking IDs: paste from Excel, Word or Notepad; scan with Enter; or type with Space/Enter. IDs separate and duplicates are removed.

Manager checks before approving Cost · planning · responsibility
  • Validate the need: compare volume, available vehicles, breakdown/service status, recent ad hoc spend and estimated CPS. Return requests that are unclear.
  • Target zero avoidable ad hocs: plan maintenance and services in advance. Combine routes and consolidate future-dated packages only where delivery commitments allow.
  • Own the cost: each additional expense raises station CPS and reduces P&L margin.
  • Brief UTR and the partner: ensure the correct date and reason are recorded. Tell the ODCD associate or van vendor that rent for absence/breakdown days will be deducted during payment settlement.
Estimated vs actual — what needs attention? Report · MTD · Excel

Use Reports → Estimated vs Actual Expenses to compare the requested estimate with the submitted actual amount, difference and percentage. Filter by period, station and payment head; follow up on overruns and missing actuals.

Example: ₹1,200 estimated → ₹1,800 actual = ₹600 over estimate (+50%).

The daily ad hoc report includes the report-day and MTD comparison, plus station-wise, day-level Excel detail. Latest saved MTD values include late updates to earlier days.

Actuals pending are identified separately. Submitted actuals do not mean paid amounts. Operations recipients receive their mapped station scope.

What should each approval level check?
  • First approver: verify the station’s reason, available vehicles, affected partner and shipment requirement.
  • Business / National Head: review the estimate, CPS, recent ad hoc usage and earlier approval comments.
  • Company replacement: Fleet Manager checks the owned vehicle’s breakdown/service status before Business Head review.

Vehicle types, in plain language

Own · ODCD · Van Rented · Van Vendor
  • Own: a DropX-owned vehicle.
  • ODCD: own-van delivery-cum-Delivery Associate — the DA owns and drives the van and delivers shipments.
  • Van Rented: a hired vehicle with the DA arranged separately.
  • Van Vendor: an external supplier providing the contracted vehicle service.
