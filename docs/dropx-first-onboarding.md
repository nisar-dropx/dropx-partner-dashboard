# DropX-first Workforce onboarding

The Workforce onboarding queue is keyed by canonical Workforce UUID, displaying its DropX ID. Managed invitations without a canonical profile remain visible as “Invited · DropX ID pending”; promotion retains the same UUID and replaces that row, rather than adding another person. Promoted invitations follow their current canonical station. Name or phone similarity never establishes ownership.

Amazon-only records are excluded from this queue. The secondary Amazon status desk retains its provider onboarding, active, offboarded and IDfy views. Its cards flag missing, conflicting or unavailable DropX ownership checks. Several explicitly linked Amazon profiles belong inside one DropX record.

DropX employment status drives Onboarding, Active, Inactive and Offboarded. Needs action overlaps those queues for incomplete registration, identity conflicts, failed invitations, mapping/rate-card setup and linked verification follow-ups. Amazon activation does not activate a DropX profile or complete registration.

Expanding a record shows registration review, DropX ID creation, DropX activation, payroll ID mapping and current rate-card configuration. A saved rate card means an effective current-station payment method with configured values; it is not a payout approval or a guarantee that every historical date is payable. Direct payment allocations are recognized. Cancelled, expired, future and other-station periods do not satisfy current setup. Zero-valued configured rates are valid.

First recorded delivery is read on expansion from the exact stored numeric payroll ID and station, since joining, with conflicting canonical ownership held for review. Unavailable evidence remains Not checked, rather than Complete or zero. Historical delivery records are not scanned for every card.

The screen is read-only with respect to identity reconciliation, employment and payment amounts. It displays saved evidence; it does not manufacture mappings or rate cards. Existing reconciliation remains responsible for exact source links and payment configuration remains with authorized operations/finance actions.

Access continues through the `workforce_onboarding` master permission. No names or special account IDs grant access in code. Existing Amazon-reference permissions are independent.
